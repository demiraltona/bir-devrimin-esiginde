About the book

A collection of the essay series “Bir Devrimin Eşiğinde” (On the Threshold of a Revolution), written between November 2017 and January 2018. The series examines the world-scale revolution shaped by artificial intelligence and robots entering every field of production. To understand this revolution it turns to the question of what the human being and society are: the place of labour, fire and tools in the process of becoming human, the errors of Engels and Kıvılcımlı, and the concepts of the realm of necessity and the realm of freedom.

The book also contains two further parts: a critique of Yuval Noah Harari’s Sapiens, and the political economy of Bitcoin.
